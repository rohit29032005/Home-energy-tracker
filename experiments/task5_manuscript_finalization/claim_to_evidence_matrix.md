## Final scientific boundary

The paper is an implementation and reproducibility study. Its strongest supported empirical statements are (1) exact offline reproduction of a legacy CSV-specific baseline and (2) deterministic, checksum-traceable REFIT preprocessing. The proposed end-to-end REFIT experiment, aggregate anomaly evaluation, alert-deduplication verification under replay, and performance/resilience study are still open.
